INNOOTTRUVAR WEBSITE


The initial objectives are to consolidate and share all useful information relevant to our community so that it will be a repository for reference.

The website is currently evolving and adding features to cover the following


  • Information about our Emmampoondi Innoottru Chettimaar Sabai and its Orgnaistaional Structure

  • Details on various Management Committees

  • Information about our Community Palakkad Vittruniyar Temple and Kula Deiva Temples and other temples

  • Devotional Songs

  • How to do Guides for various stages in the Marriage and associated Seerkal in our community

  • Our Community Oonjal song along with Checklist of Materials - both bride and bride groom house- for Marriage

  • How to do Guides for other functions and Seerkal

  • How to do Guides for celebrating various festivals according to our community culture

  • How to do Guides for Last Rites and associated functions and Checklist of Materials

  • Important Contact details
